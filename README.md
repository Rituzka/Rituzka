### 🎬 What I'm building

**[Serie Club](https://www.serie.club)** is your personal TV series guide.
Pick your shows and get notified of every new episode and season.
You can also find where to watch any series, plus trailers, trending rankings and member ratings.

- Installable web app (PWA) with push and email alerts
- Available in Spanish (Spain and Argentina), English and Finnish
- Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Vercel, using TMDB and JustWatch data
